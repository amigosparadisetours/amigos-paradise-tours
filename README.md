# Your website: how it works

Hi Darshi! This is your website, **amigosparadisetours.com**. Everything on it lives here, in your GitHub account, and GitHub publishes it for free. It is yours: you can change it yourself, without depending on anybody.

## Three ways to change your website

### 1. Free, by yourself: your panel (most changes)

Open **app.pagescms.org**, tap **Sign in with GitHub** and choose **amigos-paradise-tours**. There you can change, add or remove:

- **Main photos**: the photo at the top, About me, the three "How we'll travel" cards, the three car photos.
- **Reviews**: new reviews, their translations and their photos.
- **Photos with travellers**.
- **Places along the way**: the row of photos of places under the routes, with their names.
- **Routes**: the route ideas, drawn on the map by themselves.
- **Questions (FAQ)**: the questions and your answers, in Spanish and English.
- **Before, on arrival and during the trip**: the lists of what you do for your travellers.
- **Links**: the link to your Google reviews. Add it when Google verifies your profile, and the button appears on the website.
- **The car (details)**: passengers, luggage, water, larger groups, and the sentence about music.

Tap **Save** and the website updates in one or two minutes. You can upload photos straight from your phone: big photos are made smaller by themselves. Your guide (PDF) explains every step.

### 2. Free, with help: the Claude app without paying

The free Claude app can't change the website, but it can help you do it from your panel:

- Open a new chat, **attach your guide (PDF)** and ask, for example: *"Using this guide, how do I change the photo of the car boot?"*
- Translate a review: *"Translate this review into Spanish from Spain. Keep the meaning exactly, don't improve it or add anything: …"*

### 3. Paid only the month you need it: Claude Pro (changes in the code)

Only for things the panel can't do: changing the other texts (the top of the page, About me, the cards), adding the video, a new section, or a new place on the route map. You may never need it.

1. Pay **Claude Pro for one month**. Cancel it when you finish: you keep it until the end of the month, and the website keeps working without it.
2. Open the Claude app › **Code** › choose **amigos-paradise-tours**.
3. Write what you want, in simple English. For example: *"Change the text in About me: …"* or *"Add my video to the reviews"*.
4. Claude explains what it will change, changes it in Spanish and English and opens a **pull request**.
5. Open the pull request (GitHub app or github.com) and tap **Merge**. The website updates in about a minute.
6. If something is wrong, tell Claude: *"Undo the last change"*, and merge again.

Claude follows the rules in `CLAUDE.md`, so the website keeps your real words and your style. Please don't delete that file.

## Your friend (or any web developer)

Invite him in GitHub: this project › **Settings** › **Collaborators** › **Add people**. Everything he needs is here: `CLAUDE.md` explains the rules and how the code works. The website is one simple page (`index.html`) plus the files in `data/` that your panel changes. No special programs are needed.

## If something goes wrong

- **Nothing is ever lost:** GitHub keeps every previous version.
- **A change doesn't appear:** wait 2–3 minutes and reload. On your phone, try with mobile data instead of wifi.
- **A mistake in the panel:** open the same entry, fix it and tap **Save** again.
