# Your website: how it works

Hi Darshi! This is your website, **amigosparadisetours.com**. Everything on it lives here, in your GitHub account, and GitHub publishes it for free.

## It works on its own

You don't need to do anything to keep it working. Nothing on it expires: there are no prices or dates that go out of date. Your new reviews will be on Google and your new photos on Instagram, and the website links to both.

The only cost is the domain name, once a year. Keep automatic renewal on, so you never lose the name.

## When you want to change something

You need the **Claude app** on your phone. The **Code** tab needs a paid plan (Pro). You can pay for one month when you want changes and cancel it afterwards: the website keeps working without it.

1. Open the Claude app and tap **Code**.
2. Choose the repository **amigos-paradise-tours**.
3. Write what you want, in simple English. For example:
   - *"Answer the FAQ about tips: …"*
   - *"Add this review from Laura, Madrid, couple, 10 days: …"*
   - *"Add a route: family with two kids, 11 days. Sigiriya, Kandy, Ella, Yala, Mirissa."*
   - *"The car has 4 passenger seats and space for 3 big suitcases."*
   - *"Use img/car-boot.jpg as the photo of the car boot."*
4. Claude explains what it will change, changes it in Spanish and English, and opens a **pull request**.
5. Open the pull request (in the GitHub app or on github.com) and tap **Merge**. The website updates in about a minute.
6. Look at amigosparadisetours.com. If something is wrong, tell Claude: *"Undo the last change"*, and merge again.

Claude follows the rules in `CLAUDE.md`, so the website keeps your real words and your style. Please don't delete that file.

## When you want to change a photo

1. Give the photo a clear name, for example `car-boot.jpg`. Send the original photo, not one that went through WhatsApp.
2. On your phone, open github.com in Chrome, go to this repository and open the **img** folder.
3. Tap **Add file** › **Upload files**, choose the photo and tap **Commit changes**. If you don't see "Add file", open the Chrome menu and turn on **Desktop site**.
4. In Claude, say which photo to replace, for example: *"Use img/car-boot.jpg as the photo of the car boot."*

Uploading a photo doesn't change the website by itself: it only changes when Claude uses it.

## What is still missing

Open **amigosparadisetours.com/?revisar**. The yellow notes show what's still missing: photos to replace, answers to write. Visitors never see these notes. Without `?revisar`, you see the website exactly as visitors do.
